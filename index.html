📖 How to Play

Classic Connect

Roll the dice. Read and select a word in the matching row. Connect three words horizontally, vertically, or diagonally for a point. Every newly completed three-word line earns one point.

Trophy Race

Both teams may claim the same word. Create a connected path from the first word column to a trophy in the last column. Claiming a reachable trophy earns your team one point; each trophy can score once per team.

The team with the most points wins.
