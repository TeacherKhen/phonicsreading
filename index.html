🎲 How to Play

Board Style 1: Connect Three

1. Roll the dice to select a row.
2. Read a phonics word in that row.
3. Click the word to claim it for your team.
4. Teams take turns.
5. Connect three words horizontally, vertically, or diagonally to earn one point.

Board Style 2: Trophy Race

Both teams may claim the same words. Connect a path from the leftmost word column to the trophy on the right to earn a point. Each trophy may be claimed once per team.

The team with the most points wins.
